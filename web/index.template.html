Methodology

verified

The row matches one official document, and the link opens that document.

derived

The value is computed or read from one or more filings. The method is stated on the row or beside the figure.

partial

The source has a known gap. The row says what is missing.

Sources

Department of Justice FARA eFile bulk data, Lobbying Disclosure Act filings from LDA.gov, and the Department of Education file of Section 117 public records. @@SOURCE_DATES@@

Why Qatar leads

Qatar is the lead focus of this site. The database itself covers every country in the Justice Department file, and every country is held to the same rules.

FARA dollar amounts

The Justice Department bulk file lists who is registered for whom, with dates. It carries no dollar amounts. Amounts are shown only where they have been read from a supplemental statement, and those rows are marked derived.

FARA payments

Because the bulk file has no money in it, payment figures are read from the Item 14(a) receipts table of each registrant’s six-month supplemental statement, and each one links to the statement it came from. @@FEE_NOTE@@ Three kinds of statement yield nothing and are not guessed at: one filed as a scanned image with no machine-readable text; one whose table says “See Appendix for Response”, where the detail sits in free text that is not parsed; and one with no payment lines. A statement covers every foreign principal the registrant acts for, so the total printed on the form is never used here. Lines naming a principal from another country are read but not published: these statements were gathered because the registrant acts for one of the featured countries, so the payments they show for other countries are a fragment rather than that country’s total, and a fragment shown as a total would mislead. Those countries’ figures appear once their own statements have been read.

FARA source links

A FARA row links to the exhibit that names the foreign principal. Where the Justice Department document index has no exhibit under that exact name, the row is marked partial and links to the list of every document filed under that registration number. @@PARTIAL_NOTE@@ @@DEAD_NOTE@@

What a country means on a lobbying filing

On an LDA record, the country is where the client or a listed foreign entity is located, as written on the filing. It does not mean the filing involves that country's government.

Countries as filed

Country names are kept as the filing gives them. Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan are listed separately and are not merged into China.

Years

A FARA registration counts toward every year from its start date to its end date, or to the present if the Justice Department file lists no end date. A lobbying filing counts toward its filing year. Rows with no usable date appear only when all years are selected.

Amended reports

When a lobbying report is amended, the most recently posted version for the same registrant, client and period is used in totals. Earlier versions stay in the records and are marked as replaced.

Individuals

People appear only where a filing names them, in the detail of that filing's row. The Justice Department file does not tie an individual to a specific foreign principal, so individuals are not listed by country.

University gifts and contracts

Under Section 117 of the Higher Education Act, institutions report foreign gifts and contracts worth $250,000 or more in a year. The figures are self-reported by the institutions. The Department of Education file does not name the foreign source for most transactions, so that column is usually empty. These records run through February 28, 2025; later reports are published on the Department’s newer portal and are not yet included. A university row links to the Department’s spreadsheet, which holds every record, and not to a document for that single transaction.

Not yet included

Campaign finance records and nonprofit tax filings are not in this release. University reports for countries other than the five featured ones are also not included.

Senate Office of Public Records cannot vouch for the data or analyses derived from these data after the data have been retrieved from LDA.gov.
